# Video script (≈ 2 minutes) - optional, but it helps "Data Presentation"

The criterion also evaluates explaining findings "to a non-technical audience". Speak to camera or over a screen recording; avoid acronyms.

| Time | Screen | Suggested text |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0:00–0:15 | Summary (title "Not when they leave. When they come in.") | "When does an NBA player stop performing? Everybody thinks: when they get tired. We checked 872 thousand shots, and the answer is: not when they leave, but when they come in." |
| 0:15–0:40 | Summary (cards −1.46 and −27) and Evidence → shot type and free throws | "In their first 30 seconds on court, players shoot one and a half points below their normal level. That costs each team about 27 points a season, almost one win. It is worst in threes and mid-range, and it even shows in free throws, where there is no defender." |
| 0:40–0:55 | Evidence → Preregistered replication on 2025-26 | "Before looking at the 2025-26 season, which our model never saw, we wrote down what we expected and how we would measure it. The result: minus 1.35 points. It held." |
| 0:55–1:25 | 3D simulator: raise the minutes from 2 to 12, then bring the defender close | "And fatigue? Watch: I raise the minutes on court from 2 to 12 and the probability barely moves. I bring the defender closer and it changes everything. A Curry three goes from 37% with the defender far away to 23% with the defender on top of him." |
| 1:25–1:45 | Summary → placebo chart (60% / 59% and 0%) | "Why trust this? A popular rule, 'substitute if accuracy drops 3 points', flags 60% of players. With the minutes shuffled at random, where fatigue cannot exist, it still flags 59%. That is noise. Ours flags zero." |
| 1:45–2:00 | Tools → Calculator (three right after entering) and For the coach (card) | "In the calculator, a three 15 seconds after entering loses almost two points; a cut to the rim, less than one. Three actions: an easy first play for the player coming in, don't pull your best shooter out of fear of fatigue, and spread minutes on the second night of a back-to-back. Thank you." |

Tips: record at 1080p, visible cursor, calm pace. The simulator figure (37% → 23%) is a top-of-the-key Curry three with the defender at 8 ft and at 1.5 ft; check it on screen before recording. Say "about 27 points" and "almost one win": both are estimates with a margin (19 to 36 points). Do not present the team audit as a ranking: teams do not differ beyond noise.
